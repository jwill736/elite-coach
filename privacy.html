Privacy Policy

Elite Coach OS · last updated September 24, 2026

Elite Coach OS is a personal app with a single user: its owner. It is not offered to anyone else.

Google Calendar access

The app uses Google Calendar access only to create, update and read training events and reminders on the owner's own calendar.

Where data goes

All data stays on the owner's own computer. Nothing is sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or sent to any other server.

Removing access

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
